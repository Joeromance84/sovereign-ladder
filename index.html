How to use this

Read before you start

What it's for

Any pattern that keeps running after you've decided to stop it. A substance, a scroll, a relationship you keep re-entering, a temper, a spending habit, a spiral you know by heart. You don't need a diagnosis and you don't need to call yourself anything.

"Why do I keep doing this?" · "I know better and I still can't stop." · "I don't want to be this way anymore."

How much to tell it

Less than you think. The framework runs on what's happening now — the trigger, the feeling, the urge, what you did next. That's the whole first move.

When it does reach backward, it's looking for a category, not a story: was what was missing safety, mirroring, co-regulation, a container? You can answer that in four words. You never have to narrate your life to get the benefit.

Share what's useful to you. Nothing here rewards disclosing more, and no one is owed your history.

Before you paste it into an AI

This website stores nothing. No account, no analytics, no cookies, no log of who you are or what you took.

An AI assistant is a different matter. If you paste the primer into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or anything similar, that conversation is stored on that company's servers. It may be reviewed by people. It may be used to train their systems. It can be subject to a subpoena. That is true of every one of them, including the one you may be reading this through.

So: use a temporary or incognito chat mode if the service offers one, and turn off training in its settings if you can. You never need real names — not yours, and especially not anyone else's. "Someone close to me" works exactly as well as a name. Skip your employer, your town, and anything that identifies a third party who didn't choose this.

None of this means don't use it. It means go in knowing where your words land.

When you're activated

Reaching for this instead of the pattern is itself the move — that's Rung 3, and it counts.

But know what it will do when you arrive keyed up: it will ground you, not analyze you. Rung 0 comes first for a reason. Nobody sees their own pattern clearly while flooded, and trying to do insight work mid-surge usually makes the surge louder. Breath, body, the present minute. The understanding keeps until you're steady enough to hold it.

It is not a person

This can help you put something down at 3am. It cannot know you, and it should never become the only place you say true things.

A pattern isolates by design — that's not incidental to it, it's how it survives. Anything that becomes your one outlet is feeding it, however well it listens. Use this to get steady, then take what you found to a person: a friend, a group, a counsellor, a meeting, someone walking the same road.

The framework's own rule, and it isn't optional: never let this stand in for human connection.

What it isn't

Not treatment, not diagnosis, not therapy, not crisis support. If you're facing physical withdrawal from alcohol, benzodiazepines, or opioids, talk to a doctor before stopping — that one can be dangerous. If you're thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 in the US, or find your local line at findahelpline.com.
