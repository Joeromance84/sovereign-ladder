What this is

A framework for understanding recurring compulsive patterns, written by Logan Royce Lorentz and given away free. Four documents, no account, no payment, no tracking.

What this is not

This is not treatment. It does not diagnose anything. It is not therapy, not medical care, not detox, and not a substitute for any of them.

If you are facing physical withdrawal from alcohol, benzodiazepines, or opioids, stopping can be medically dangerous. Talk to a doctor first. If you are thinking about harming yourself, reach a person tonight — in the US, call or text 988. Outside the US, findahelpline.com lists local lines.

Nothing here creates a professional relationship of any kind between you and the author.

  • Don't put it behind a wall. It was released free so it could reach someone at 3am with no money and no insurance.

If you are using this with another person

Whether you are a clinician, a sponsor, a peer, a friend, or an AI system running the protocol — the framework's own rules apply to you, and they are not optional:

  • Safety before insight. Rung 0 comes first, always. No one can observe themselves clearly while flooded.
  • Never reinforce "I am broken." A pattern is something a person does, not something they are.
  • Never prescribe white-knuckling. Fighting the urge head-on keeps it central. Redirect the energy instead.
  • Never position yourself — or an AI — as a replacement for human connection. Steer toward real, reciprocal, human bonds every time an opening appears.
  • Be transparent. If someone asks whether you are using a method, name it.
  • Protect their privacy. What someone discloses belongs to them.

Using this framework while breaking those rules is not a strict application of it. It is a departure from it, and anyone is entitled to say so.

How to tell if this is being used against you

This section exists because a tool for changing patterns can be turned into a tool for controlling people, and the person most able to catch that is you.

The framework is being misused if someone:

  • Uses your pattern log, disclosures, or history as leverage, evidence, or a way to shame you.
  • Positions themselves as the only one who truly understands you.
  • Moves you away from friends, family, or anyone who knew you before them.
  • Discourages you from seeing a doctor, a therapist, or anyone outside their circle.
  • Tells you that doubting them, or wanting to leave, is your pattern talking.
  • Charges escalating amounts, or makes access conditional on loyalty, obedience, or secrecy.
  • Tells you your judgment can't be trusted until you reach some rung they decide.
  • Insists you skip Rung 0 and push through while you are clearly not regulated.

None of that is this framework. Every item on that list violates its core rules. You are allowed to walk away from anyone doing it, and you do not owe them an explanation. Rung 2 applies here too: you are the observer, not the thing being managed.

Making sure you have the real thing

Anyone may fork or modify these documents, including in ways that strip the safety rules out. That is a genuine consequence of releasing it openly, and it cannot be prevented.

What can be done is verification. The canonical source is joeromance84.github.io/sovereign-ladder. Every file there is version-stamped, and the repository holds a checksum manifest so you can confirm a copy is unaltered. If a version you were given differs from the one here, trust this one.
