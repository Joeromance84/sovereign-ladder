Print sheet — 8 cards per page

Eight cards at standard business-card size (3.5″ × 2″) on US Letter. The QR is the vector file from this repository — nothing is fetched from any outside server, so these cards work forever and leak nothing.

  • Home printing: hit print, choose Actual size (not "fit to page"), then cut along the dashed guides.
  • Print shop: don't send them this page. Send them assets/qr-code.svg — a vector file is what they actually want, and it stays sharp at any size.
  • Card stock beats paper. 65–110 lb. Cards get carried; paper gets thrown away.
  • Scan one before you cut the rest.
